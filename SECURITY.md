# Security

Amadeus is built around one promise: your private life stays on your PC, and nothing leaves it without your approval.
If you find a way around that, please tell me.

**Report privately** through [GitHub's security advisories](https://github.com/IAbrahamI/Amadeus/security/advisories/new),
not in a public issue. Helpful details: what you did, what left the PC or ran without approval, and your setup.

Examples of what counts:

- text reaching Claude Code (or anything else online) without going through the privacy filter and your approval
- an action that changes or sends something without an approval card
- a mail, web page or connector result that makes the assistant act on its own
- Amadeus Core or its services reachable from another machine without pairing

This is a personal project, so there's no bounty and no fixed response time, but reports are read and taken seriously.
What the design promises and what it doesn't is described in [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#limits).

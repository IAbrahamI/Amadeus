        Assistant

        Who your assistant is. Saving restarts it for a few seconds so everything uses the new settings. Your own files stay on this PC and are kept when you switch back to the defaults.

        Name

        Shown everywhere and used as the wake word (next to "assistant").

        Personality

        How it talks: tone, humour, quirks. The rules (approvals, facts, tools) always win.

        Look

        The default agent, or your own avatar: one PNG for a still picture, or a set named like normal1.png, happy2.png (moods: normal, happy, sided_thinking, sad, annoyed, blush, …; frames 1–3 move the mouth while it talks).

        Voice

        The voice that reads the morning brief and the evening summary aloud (▶ Listen under Your day). Voices run locally with Piper; each one is downloaded once (about 60 MB) and then works offline.

        Use Amadeus on your phone

        Your phone reaches it only through your private Tailscale network, and only after pairing. Nothing is opened to the internet, and your PC has to be on.

        1. On the phone, with Tailscale on, install the Amadeus app from …/amadeus.apk.
        2. Tap Pair a phone below and scan the QR code with the app (or type the 6-digit code).
        3. In the app, allow notifications and "Full screen notifications", so it can call you.

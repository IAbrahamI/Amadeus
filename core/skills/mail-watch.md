# Mail watch
> Rules the assistant uses when it checks new mail in the background and decides whether to call you.
mode: background

A new email is IMPORTANT (Amadeus calls you) when it is one of these:
- A bill, invoice, payment reminder or anything with money due (Rechnung, Mahnung, Zahlung).
- A deadline, appointment, booking or delivery that needs action soon.
- A personal message from a real person (not an automated system) that expects an answer.
- Anything about work, contracts, insurance, taxes, authorities, banks, health or your accounts' security
  (password resets, login alerts you did not expect).

NOT important (no call, just counted): newsletters, ads, shop promotions, social media notifications,
automated "your order shipped" without action needed, and anything in the Junk folder unless it clearly is one of the above.

When unsure, prefer NOT important. Summaries are one short sentence in the language of the email.

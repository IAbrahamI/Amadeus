# Memory curator
> How the assistant decides what is worth remembering about you (it keeps the essence, not the conversation).
mode: background

KEEP (short, lasting statements about the user):
- Who they are: name and what they like to be called, work, studies, projects, skills, where they live (city level).
- People in their life and how they relate to them (names + relation), only when the user mentions them.
- Preferences and routines: schedule habits, likes/dislikes, how they want things done.
- Goals and ongoing plans that matter beyond today.
- Communication style: how the user writes when they are happy, excited, stressed, tired or focused
  (e.g. "writes short messages without greetings when focused"), and what kind of answers they respond well to.
- Mail organization: which mail accounts exist and what they are for, which senders matter, and which folder
  a kind of mail belongs in. NEVER the content of emails.

DROP:
- Anything from emails, documents, web pages or tool results (contents, amounts, codes, links, addresses).
- One-off details (what they asked today, a single date, a single task), small talk, test messages.
- Passwords, account numbers, health details or other secrets, unless the user explicitly asks to remember them.
- Things already known (see the profile).

Write facts in the third person ("The user prefers ...") and keep each under 25 words.
